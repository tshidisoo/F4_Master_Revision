Unit 1 · Present Simple and Present Continuous

Present simplePresent continuous
I play football after school.I'm playing football right now.
You go to school by bus.You're on the bus. You 're going to school.
She reads magazines at the weekend.She's reading a book for school.
Write.
  1. We usually play football on Wednesdays, but we 're playing volleyball today.
  2. You never drink orange juice.
  3. I must take an umbrella. It 's raining.
Rewrite with the correct time marker.
  1. She plays tennis on Sundays (always/now). She always plays tennis on Sundays.
  2. They're cooking dinner (sometimes/right now). They're cooking dinner right now.
  3. We go to restaurants (rarely/today). We rarely go to restaurants.

Unit 3 · Past Simple: Irregular Verbs — Negatives and Questions

AffirmativeNegative / Question
You went to the party.I didn't go to the party.
She made her bed,but he didn't make his bed.
I went to the museum yesterday.Did you go to a museum yesterday?
He saw a street festival last night.What did he see at the festival?
Rewrite in the negative.
  1. I heard the music. I didn't hear the music.
  2. We saw a robot. We didn't see a robot.
  3. She bought a model. She didn't buy a model.
Write the questions.
  1. What / they / about the concert? What did they think about the concert? — They thought it was great.
  2. What / she / at the party? What did she wear at the party? — She wore a long red dress.

Read and circle.
  1. Are these his / ours trainers?

Unit 5 · have to / had to & why / because

PresentPast
I'm going to be late. Sorry, I have to go now.We forgot the map. We had to buy another one.
He's at the red light. He has to stop.She had to stay late, because she didn't do her homework.
Write.
  1. We're lost. We have to read the map.
  2. I was at the theatre early, so I had to wait.
Match (why / because).
  1. Why are they late? Because they got lost.
  2. Why is he wet? Because he forgot his umbrella.
  3. Why are you going to the park? Because I like playing outside.

Affirmative and negativeQuestions and short answers

Write.

  2. Monday, 23rd February 2009 → on Monday
  3. Saturday, 21st March 2009 → in a month's time

Unit 8 · Expressing Quantity & Some/Any

Countable nounsUncountable nouns

But he's got lots of coins.Look! They've got lots of luggage.
Have you got any newspapers?Have you got any butter?

Read and circle.
  1. How many / much oranges would you like?
  2. My friend Danny hasn't got much / many money.
  3. How much / many luggage have you got?

  1. He's got / magazines. He's got some magazines.
  2. Is there / sun cream? Is there any sun cream?
  3. Have you got / pastries? Have you got any pastries?

Unit 10 · Present Perfect: Affirmative, Negative, Questions

AffirmativeI've saved the document. You've logged on. He's cleaned his teeth. We 've made a mess.
NegativeI haven't seen your computer. She hasn't done her homework. They haven't finished the test.
QuestionsHave you seen our computer? Has she done her homework? Have they finished the test?
Write (tidy, finish, log on).
  1. The room looks great! She 's tidied it.
  2. I 've finished my homework. Can I go out now?
  3. You can use the computer now. I 've logged on for you.
Write the dialogue.

Andy: Can I watch TV now, Dad?
Dad: Have you finished all your homework?
Andy: Yes, I have, and I've put all my books away.
Dad: Have you turned off the computer?
Andy: No, I haven't. Jane hasn't finished her homework, so it's still on.

Unit 15 · Past Simple vs Past Continuous & there/they're/their

They were printing their homework when the printer broke. When it started to rain, we were swimming in the lake.
There — a place, or what is presentThere is a new stadium in our city.
They're — short for "they are"They're all photos from my website.
Their — shows possession (more than one person)Their names are Su and Jess.
Circle.
  1. The boys were playing football when it stopped raining.
  2. We were waiting in the car when the author walked past.
Write there, they're or their.

"Sam and Alex invited their friends to a party. 'Where are Jack, Alice and Olly?' asked Sam. 'Olly is over there,' said Mum. 'And Jack and Alice are in their car. They're on the way.'"
